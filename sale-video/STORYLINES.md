## Picked: A + B, "One system, a new game every season"
Why (PK): A shows how many games one system can make. B is even stronger because it makes the merchant picture seasonal and festival marketing, which helps sell.

**Vertical (PK, 19-11-2026):** 9:16, 1080x1920, 30 fps, about 45 to 50 s. Faster than the landscape plan: a new beat every 2 to 4 s.
Festival stops and the final count come from PK's sample game library (location to be confirmed).

Vertical layout rules:
- One subject at a time, centred and stacked top to bottom. Landscape ideas (side-by-side windows, a sideways calendar) are rebuilt vertically.
- All text and games stay out of the top 170 px and the bottom 250 px, where LINE / Telegram draw their player controls. If the clip is later posted to TikTok / Reels, keep text above 1330 px instead.
- Large type: at most about 5 words per line, nothing under 28 px. Cut words, never shrink.
- On the desktop sales page, the vertical video sits in a phone-shaped frame beside the page copy (one video for every channel).

Confidentiality rule (PK's manager, 19-11-2026): **no back office or settings screen anywhere in the video**, and no on-screen explanation of how a game is built (no "upload 3 images", no stage names). Competitors must not learn how the system works. Show only what the player sees and what the merchant gets.

## The one thing
- **What it is:** one Minigames system can become any "pick and reveal" game, plus a fully re-skinnable wheel. No extra system to buy for each new game.
- **What the viewer should remember (in their words):** "One system does the box, the cards, the fortune sticks and more. I don't need to buy a separate system per game."
- **Who watches:** merchants on AUTO FAST / LSM-SYSTEM. Many don't know the product exists, or think every vendor's minigames are the same.
- **Spine:** the same player screen becomes a new game for every season. How it is set up is never shown (confidentiality rule above).

## Facts the film may claim (checked against the original back office captures)
- Pick game: 3 / 6 / 9 choices. 3 image stages: before pick, on pick, result. Stage 2 can be skipped.
- Each choice can use a different image. Choice order can be shuffled.
- Upload hint accepts png, gif, jpg, so stage 2 can be an animated GIF (e.g. shaking fortune sticks). Checked from the settings hint only, not yet tested on the player side.
- Wheel: 6 / 8 / 10 slices. Every part's image can be changed (frame, spin button, win pop-up, slice images). 28 theme colours.
- Several games can run at once, each with its own rules.
- Supporting points (short, after the highlight): rights granted automatically from deposits; budget, per-player and stock caps; mission rewards that grant game rights.

## Claims that need care
- "Other vendors sell one system per game" is PK's market knowledge, not verified by us. Never name or show a real competitor.
- "Endless forms" is true for the look only. The mechanics are "pick one and reveal" and "spin". Show only forms that fit those mechanics (box, cards, fortune sticks, red envelopes, eggs, gacha capsules). No slot machines or scratch cards.

## A. "One box, every game" (recommended)
Recommended because it says PK's exact message in the first ten seconds (it opens on the three games everyone knows, then keeps going) and reads with the sound off.

- **Device / form:** the feature as the hook / match cuts (one shape holds while everything around it changes).
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** Three purple mystery boxes sit centre stage. Each time one opens, all three snap into a different game (cards, fortune sticks, red envelopes, eggs, gacha capsules) while the grid never moves, and the viewer thinks "wait, that's all the same thing?" before being shown the three upload slots that make it happen.
- **First two seconds:** Frame 0 is three purple boxes on a deep purple ground, the middle one already shaking. At 0.6 s its lid pops and "เครดิต 100" rises out. At 1.4 s the three boxes match-cut into three face-down cards, and the label "กล่องสุ่ม" flips to "เปิดไพ่".
- **Beats:** hook (box → cards → fortune sticks) → tension: "ที่อื่น 1 ระบบ = 1 เกม", three plain unnamed windows with one game each and a fourth slot reading "ซื้อเพิ่ม" → reveal: "ระบบเดียว" → proof: the real settings panel, 3 images uploaded (ก่อนเลือก / ตอนเลือก / ผลลัพธ์), the game turns into fortune sticks with a shaking GIF → payoff: an accelerating match-cut run through PK's 20+ real sample games plus a re-skinned wheel, landing on a wall of all of them → also: 3 quick shots (deposit → rights arrive automatically; caps and stock; mission reward → game ticket) → claim: "ระบบเดียว เปลี่ยนเป็นเกมไหนก็ได้" → end card (Minigames mark + call to action).
- **Look:** deep purple ground and gold, taken from the existing promo art (gold-rim wheel with bulbs, purple box). Thai sans-serif. The one material: glossy gold rim.
- **Sound:** bright pop-funk bed around 115 bpm entering strong. A pop on each reveal, whooshes on the match cuts (2 to 3 rotating takes), one riser into the 3x3 grid payoff.
- **Length:** about 45 s.

## B. "A year of festivals"
- **Device / form:** a moment in someone's day (an admin planning the year's promos) / a flight through one world (a calendar strip the camera travels along).
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** An admin's year: Chinese New Year, Songkran, Halloween, Loy Krathong, Christmas. At each stop the same game gets three new images and becomes that festival's game, and the viewer, who runs these promos every year, thinks "every festival needs a new game, and I wouldn't need a new system."
- **First two seconds:** Frame 0 is a calendar page "ตรุษจีน" with a sticky note "ต้องมีเกมใหม่!". At 1.0 s the camera dives into the note.
- **Beats:** hook (deadline note) → tension: price tags "ระบบเกมใหม่ = ซื้อใหม่" stacking up on the calendar → reveal: one Minigames panel → proof: upload 3 images → fortune sticks for Chinese New Year → payoff: the camera flies along the year and the game re-skins itself in place at every month, each festival a different form → also: 3 quick shots → claim: "เกมใหม่ทุกเทศกาล ในระบบเดียว" → end card.
- **Look:** a purple home stage with one bright colour field per festival. Thai sans-serif. The one material: a calendar page edge.
- **Sound:** light festive percussion around 100 bpm, one small instrument colour per festival, a pop on each re-skin.
- **Length:** about 55 s.
- **Risk:** festivals can read as "just new themes" instead of "new games", so each festival must use a different form.

## C. "The game machine" (the bold one)
- **Device / form:** the object is the explanation / one continuous take, the camera never cuts.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** A big gold machine has three slots labelled ก่อนเลือก / ตอนเลือก / ผลลัพธ์. Image cards drop into the slots, the machine churns, and a playable game slides out onto a conveyor that the camera follows as more and more games come out; it feels playful and toy-like, and the mechanism is the message.
- **First two seconds:** Frame 0 is a close-up of the machine with a box image card dropping into slot 1. At 1.2 s a mystery box game pops out of the chute.
- **Beats:** hook (a box comes out) → tension: tiny machines beside it, each stamped with one game, "ที่อื่น 1 เครื่อง = 1 เกม" → reveal → proof: cards, then fortune sticks, with the real settings panel shown on the machine's screen → payoff: the machine speeds up and the conveyor fills with games → also → claim → end card.
- **Look:** toy factory, gold machine on purple. The one material: brushed gold.
- **Sound:** mechanical ticks and clunks on the machine over a bouncy bed around 110 bpm.
- **Length:** about 50 s.
- **Trade-off:** the most memorable, but the most drawing work and the longest build. The real settings UI only appears on the machine's screen.
